# Search

The search field sits at the top of the sidebar. Cmd-F or Cmd-L focuses it with its text selected, and text typed after it in the same frame replaces the text.

Every edit raises the query generation and throws out queued Search jobs. Once typing has stopped for 250 ms (`SEARCH_DEBOUNCE`, a `ui.wake_at`, not a running timer), a `Search` job asks TIDAL's `search` for songs, albums, artists and playlists in one request, 50 of each. Nothing goes while the input method still has marked text, so composing にほんご doesn't search for each kana. Return searches at once, for the text it was pressed on: an edit after it in the same frame starts the debounce over, as it would in the next frame. A result whose generation isn't the latest is dropped, and the auth generation drops results from a login that has ended, as for every job. The app logs each query it sends and what came back.

The songs become the open track list, a `Track_View` with source `.Search`. So covers, double-click to play, the queue, the playing row and Enter work as in a playlist, and more songs load as `Tracks` jobs that page through `search` with only tracks, up to TIDAL's 300. The old results stay on screen until the new ones arrive. Tabs above the table switch to the albums, artists and playlists found, shown as rows. A click opens an album's tracks (`albums/{id}/tracks`) or a playlist, each as a track list of its own, or an artist's page, whose top tracks (`artists/{id}/toptracks`) are the open list (see [UI](ui.md#the-artist-page)). Picking a sidebar entry goes back to it. Clearing the field, by deleting the text or with Escape, closes the search and opens the sidebar's playlist again if the results were showing.

Opening any list, from the sidebar, the tabs or the track menu, ends a search that's still waiting for its debounce or its answer. The generation goes up and the queued Search job goes, so the answer can't bring the results back over the list. The text stays in the field, and Return searches for it again.

Signing out clears the field, the results and the history Back and Forward go through, so the next account starts with none of them.

An album result's artists are links to their pages. The track menu's Go to album and Go to artist open the same lists, from the row's album and artist ids. A song by several artists gets a Go to item for each of them in place of Go to artist. The menu offers these only when the row has the ids. Copy song link puts `https://tidal.com/browse/track/<id>` on the clipboard. The menu belongs to the list and the track it was opened on: when another list replaces that list (a search result, a sidebar entry, an album), or the row holds another track, the menu closes and its actions do nothing. Add to queue is gone from the menu until the player has a queue API to call.

With `TIDALFAST_FAKE=1` the search runs over the 10,000 fake rows on the UI thread. It matches title, artist or album, ignoring case, and its albums and artists are the distinct albums and artists of the matching songs, up to 50 of each. "Jóga" matches 145 songs by title, from 26 albums and 34 artists. Going to an album lists the fake rows with that name. Going to an artist opens its page, made from the fake rows: its rows are its top tracks, their albums its albums, EPs and singles, and the artists of the other rows on those albums the similar artists.
